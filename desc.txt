Project 2 — FlowForge

Distributed Job Scheduler with AI-Assisted Config

Pitch: A lightweight Zapier/GitHub-Actions-style platform — users define scheduled jobs or webhook-triggered workflows, a fleet of worker processes executes them, and a live dashboard streams status and logs. An AI assistant lets users describe a job in plain English ("scrape this page daily at 9am, alert me after 3 failures") and it generates the structured config; another AI feature summarizes failure patterns from raw logs instead of making the user read them. This is the project meant to prove "system design" is more than a buzzword on the resume — it forces building the actual patterns interviewers probe (queues, retries, idempotency, worker scaling), not just describing them.

Stack:

Backend: Node.js + TypeScript, Redis-backed queue driving a worker fleet.
Execution: workers run scheduled/webhook-triggered jobs with retries and idempotency; live status/log dashboard.
AI: plain-English job definitions translated into structured configs via OpenRouter; LLM-generated summaries of failure patterns from raw logs.
Containerization: Docker; workers horizontally scalable behind the queue.
